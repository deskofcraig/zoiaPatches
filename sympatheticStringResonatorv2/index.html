What it does

When a grand piano stands in the room while you play, its un-damped strings vibrate sympathetically with every note in the air. This patch recreates that effect on ZOIA. Four delay-line resonators track your pitch in real time and ring at the fundamental, octave above, perfect fifth, and octave below — the same intervals that piano strings respond to. A Haas stereo spread creates natural width without any modulation artifact. Plate reverb adds the acoustic body of the instrument and room.

The result is entirely clean and organic: no chorus, no distortion, no vibrato. Just the sensation of a piano resonating alongside you.

Signal flow (simplified)

Guitar → Audio Input — splits to dry path and wet path simultaneously.

Wet path: Gate VCA (env-follower-driven, controlled by K_SENS) → 4 parallel Delay Line resonators (each feeds its audio back through a Feedback VCA, sustain set by K_FEEDBACK) → 4-ch Audio Mixer → SV Filter (tone colour, K_PIANO) → Haas Stereo Spread (width) → Plate Reverb (space, K_REVERB) → Wet VCAs → output.

Pitch tracking: Pitch Detector → CV Filter (smooth) → CV Invert (higher pitch = shorter delay = higher resonant freq) → all 4 Delay Line time inputs. Voices are pre-set at harmonic intervals via knob positions; pitch CV moves all four together.

Footswitches (ZOIA)

  • Left — Infinite Sustain (latching): Closes the Gate VCA (no new excitation) and sets feedback to 0.95. Current resonance loops indefinitely until disengaged.
  • Center — Resonance Kill (momentary): Drops feedback to 0. Instantly silences all resonators.
  • Right — Bypass (latching): ZOIA audio bypass via Device Control module.
